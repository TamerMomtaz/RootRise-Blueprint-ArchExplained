## 11-Dimension Framework

Every sector pack contains these 11 intelligence dimensions:

| # | Dimension | Description | Primary Agent Users |
|---|-----------|-------------|---------------------|
| 1 | Industry Classification | ISIC codes, subsectors, value chain | Drucker, All |
| 2 | Financial Benchmarks | Margins, ratios, working capital | Graham |
| 3 | Operational KPIs | OEE, quality, productivity, delivery | Marvin, Ohno |
| 4 | Regulatory Landscape | Licenses, certifications, compliance | Marvin, Ricardo |
| 5 | Competitive Dynamics | Five Forces, strategies, risk matrix | Porter |
| 6 | Digital Maturity | Tech adoption, systems, automation | Lovelace |
| 7 | Workforce Norms | Compensation, skills, turnover | Mayo |
| 8 | Supply Chain | Suppliers, inventory, logistics | Ohno |
| 9 | Export Requirements | Markets, certifications, documentation | Ricardo |
| 10 | Packaging & Labeling | Market-specific requirements | Landor |
| 11 | MENA Regional Context | Country profiles, opportunities | All |
